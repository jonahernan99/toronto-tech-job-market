About the data

  • Source: the monthly Job Bank files published by Employment and Social Development Canada. Job Bank under-represents tech hiring compared with sites such as LinkedIn, so read the numbers as one official sample, not the whole market.
  • Tech means fifteen NOC 2021 occupations grouped into six families. The Toronto Region is Job Bank's economic region, which includes Mississauga, Brampton and the rest of the GTA.
  • Salaries are converted to an annual figure, and the midpoint of a range is used. Salaries with an implied hourly rate below $12 or above $250 are left out.
  • The SQL behind every rule, a written report and the code that produced this extract are in the GitHub repository. Download the data (CSV).
